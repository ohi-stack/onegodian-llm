# OLLM Plans & Monetization

Updated: 2026-09-03
Status: Pricing architecture — final prices require founder approval

## Objective

Create recurring software revenue without overcomplicating launch.

## Plan structure

### Free
Purpose: acquisition and product trial.

Recommended capabilities:
- limited monthly executions
- limited supported models
- standard synthesis
- short history retention or limited saved sessions

### Professional
Purpose: recurring individual subscription.

Recommended capabilities:
- higher monthly usage limits
- broader model access
- persistent history
- saved outputs
- export tools when implemented
- priority runtime limits where operationally supported

### Business
Purpose: teams, higher-volume professional use and API-oriented customers.

Recommended capabilities:
- higher usage limits
- team/workspace support when implemented
- API access when production-ready
- organization-level billing
- enhanced administration and usage reporting

## Billing principles

- Use actual configured plan entitlements, not UI-only labels.
- Meter costly provider operations.
- Enforce limits server-side.
- Preserve an auditable record of entitlement changes.
- Never promise unlimited usage unless economics and infrastructure support it.
- Do not publish final prices until billing, margins and provider costs have been reviewed.

## Commercial metrics

Track at minimum:
- registered accounts
- activated accounts
- weekly/monthly active users
- free-to-paid conversion
- monthly recurring revenue
- churn
- executions per user
- provider cost per execution
- gross margin by plan
- failed/partial execution rate

## 2026 business purpose

OLLM is intended to help address the company's documented operating challenges by creating recurring revenue, a measurable software customer base, demonstrable technical implementation, institutional traction evidence and workflows that reduce manual founder dependence.
