# Canon Integrity Policy

Version: 0.1.0

## Purpose

Canon files define the highest-level truth boundaries for the OHI™ / QOHI™ LLM environment.

## Policy

Canon files may be expanded, corrected, or versioned, but they must not be silently overwritten in a way that changes identity, authorship, chronology, governance boundaries, legal positioning, or public-facing authority claims.

## Required Controls

- Version every material canon update.
- Record date, author, approving authority, and reason for change.
- Preserve prior versions where practical.
- Flag public-facing changes for legal-safe review.
- Do not bind model behavior to unreviewed canon drafts in production.
